Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: Email address, password (securely hashed), and display name when you create an account.
  • Expense data: The transactions you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and any attached notes.
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited, features used, and session duration.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device information for security and analytics purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Service:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To store, display, and organize your expense data
  • To generate spending summaries, category breakdowns, and monthly reports
  • To enable data export functionality
  • To send service-related communications (account updates, security notices)
  • To improve the Service through analytics and user feedback
  • To ensure security and prevent fraud

We do not use your personal expense data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers with the following protections:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt)
  • Database access is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Automated backups with point-in-time recovery

Your expense data is retained for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services to operate Spendly:

  • Hosting provider: For server infrastructure and database hosting
  • Email service: For transactional emails (account verification, password reset)
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage analytics to improve the Service (no personal expense data included)

These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only for the purposes described above. We do not share your personal expense data with third parties for their own purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Download your expense data in a portable format (CSV/JSON)
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

You can exercise these rights through your account settings or by contacting us.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Spendly uses minimal cookies and browser storage:

  • Session cookie: To keep you signed in (essential)
  • Preferences: To remember your theme and display preferences

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies that identify you personally.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, if appropriate, by providing a prominent notice within the Service.

Your continued use of the Service after any such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the Service or visit our website.
